SPANISH LANGUAGE APP - REWRITTEN STORYLINE DIALOGUE
8 Episodes with Florencia in Buenos Aires
Authentic Argentine Dialogue for B1-B2 Spanish Learners
================================================================================

This document contains all rewritten dialogue from the 8-episode mini-series.
Each episode naturally incorporates specific Spanish Subjunctive grammar concepts
while telling an immersive story set in Buenos Aires, Argentina.

KEY MOTIFS:
- "Soñemos" by Carlos Di Sarli - The song that plays throughout the story (from Episode 2)
- Grandmother Valentina Reyes - Florencia's late grandmother, a tango legend
- Mate cup - A secret gift Florencia buys for the user
- Reality vs. Illusion - The philosophical theme woven throughout

DIALOGUE STRUCTURE:
- [PAUSE] indicates natural break for potential user interaction
- [GRAMMAR: X] annotates subjunctive usage for reference
- Each segment designed for ~6 second video clips
- Total runtime per episode: ~2 minutes

Grammar Focus: Present Subjunctive (expressing desire)

Setting: Buenos Aires' oldest café, afternoon

décor attracted legendary figures like Carlos Gardel and Jorge Luis Borges. The 
café represents the sophisticated, European-influenced side of Argentine culture.
