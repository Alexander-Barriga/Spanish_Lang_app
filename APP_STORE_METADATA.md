## Description (4000 chars max)
Spanish Lab is an immersive language learning app that teaches you Spanish subjunctive grammar through an emotionally compelling 8-episode story set in Buenos Aires.

Follow Florencia as she navigates life, love, and identity across eight richly scripted episodes. Each episode introduces a new form of the Spanish subjunctive — from present subjunctive wishes and desires, to imperfect subjunctive counterfactuals and pluperfect regrets.

**STORY-BASED LEARNING**
Every lesson is embedded in Florencia's story. You're not just memorizing conjugation tables — you're connecting grammar to real emotions, real situations, and real conversation.

**WRITING EXERCISES**
After each episode, three story-connected writing prompts challenge you to express your own wishes, regrets, and hypothetical scenarios using the grammar you just learned. An AI tutor reviews your writing and gives personalized feedback.

**AI CONVERSATIONS**
Practice speaking with an AI conversation partner that adapts to your grammar level. Build confidence using the subjunctive in real-time dialogue.

**EPISODE ARTICLES**
Deepen your understanding with companion reading articles for each episode that reinforce vocabulary, grammar patterns, and cultural context.

**WHAT YOU'LL LEARN**
- Present Subjunctive (Presente de Subjuntivo)
- Present Perfect Subjunctive (Pretérito Perfecto de Subjuntivo)
- Imperfect Subjunctive (Imperfecto de Subjuntivo)
- Pluperfect Subjunctive (Pluscuamperfecto de Subjuntivo)
- Trigger phrases, emotional expression, and real-world usage

**FREEMIUM MODEL**
Episode 1 is completely free. Subscribe for $14.99/month to unlock all 8 episodes and the full learning experience.

Download Spanish Lab and start your journey into the most expressive part of Spanish grammar.
