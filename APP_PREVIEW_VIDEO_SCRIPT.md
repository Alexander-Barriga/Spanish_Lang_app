# Spanish Lab — App Store Preview Video Script

A 28-30 second App Store preview voiceover in Florencia's voice: warm Argentine-accented English with a few sprinkled Spanish phrases, spoken in-character as the user's guide while app features show on screen.

Selling point: this is THE app to fall in love with the Spanish subjunctive through emotionally compelling, character-based story. If your goal isn't the subjunctive, this app isn't for you — and that focus is the strength.

## 3. ElevenLabs delivery settings

- **Voice:** a warm, mid-30s female Latin-American voice; if available, an Argentine/Rioplatense option. Otherwise a neutral warm Spanish-accented English voice.
- **Settings:** Stability ~40-45 (lets emotion vary), Similarity ~75, Style ~30-40, Speaker Boost on.
- **Pacing:** the ellipsis after "classroom" should land as a real pause — it's the emotional turn of the whole spot.

### Spanish pronunciation (for an English-accented voice)

- **"Quiero que sientas todo"** → *KYEH-roh keh see-EN-tahs TOH-doh* ("I want you to feel everything").
- **"Dale"** → *DAH-leh* (Argentine "let's go / alright").
- **"empecemos"** → *em-peh-SEH-mohs* ("let's begin").

If a phrase sounds off, generate the Spanish words in a separate render and splice them in, or spell them phonetically in the input box.
